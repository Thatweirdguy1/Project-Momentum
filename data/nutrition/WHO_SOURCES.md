# WHO source assessment

The supplied [WHO nutrition/food-safety database directory](https://www.who.int/teams/nutrition-and-food-safety/databases) was retrieved with Scrapling on 16 November 2026. It links population nutrition indicators, child-growth/malnutrition surveillance, micronutrient deficiency information, food additives/contaminants, pesticide evaluations and policy databases.

Those records are not food composition per 100 g. No calories, protein, carbohydrate or fat values were inferred from surveillance indicators. Use USDA/INDB food records for the food-reference layer and retain WHO links as separate public-health references. Reuse terms and indicator definitions would need review before importing a specific WHO dataset.
